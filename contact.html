Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How does GiveGo verify receiver organizations?
Every school, hospital, or NGO submitting support requests is thoroughly vetted by our Admin team before their requests are published to donors.
📦 How do physical donation handovers work?
Donors can choose between self-delivering items to receivers or offering self-pickup. Live GPS tracking stream is active during delivery.
⚡ How does the Smart AI Match Engine work?
Our algorithm automatically pairs donor surplus listings with receiver requests based on item title keywords, category, and district proximity.
🔒 Is my personal contact information protected?
Yes. Your contact information is kept private and is only shared with verified partners once a donation handover request is confirmed.
