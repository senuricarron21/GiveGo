How We Make Work

The GiveGo 4-Step Intelligent Workflow

From item listing to verified handover and transparent evidence tracking, GiveGo simplifies end-to-end charitable logistics.

1

Post & Request

Donors list surplus physical goods or monetary support. Verified receivers (Schools, Hospitals, Orphanages) submit urgent needs with quantity specifications.

2

Matches & Connections

Our intelligent matching engine pairs available surplus with active needs based on district location, urgency level, and material categories.

3

Dispatch & Live GPS

Parties coordinate via direct chat, schedule pickup appointments, or stream live GPS delivery journeys for reliable and safe handover.

4

Handover & SLA Proof

Receivers confirm delivery receipt, and for monetary aid, submit 14-day utilisation evidence (invoices and photos) to maintain 100% accountability.

Our Mission & Values

About GiveGo

Sri Lanka's dedicated platform for transparent, trackable, and verified charitable material distributions.

Why We Built GiveGo

Every year, tons of usable educational materials, medical equipment, and surplus supplies are wasted, while verified welfare institutions face shortages due to lack of coordination.

GiveGo removes intermediaries, verifies charitable recipients with strict governance checks, and introduces live GPS tracking alongside a 14-day SLA evidence upload framework.

Proof of Utilisation

Verification Standards

  • NGO Registration Validation: Every receiver organisation must submit registration proofs vetted by GiveGo administrators.
  • Direct Transparency: Donors see exactly which verified organisation receives their supplies.
  • Closed-Loop Accountability: Physical receipts and utilisation photos are audited before completion.
